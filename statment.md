# Project Statement

## Problem Statement

Managing daily expenses manually can make it difficult to keep track
of spending and monthly budgets.

## Proposed Solution

Personal Expense Tracker provides a simple Python-based solution for
recording expenses, managing budgets and analysing spending.

## Target Users

- Students
- Individuals
- Families
- Anyone who wants to track daily expenses

## Scope

The system allows users to add, view and delete expenses, set a
monthly budget, check budget status and analyse expenses.

## Main Functional Areas

1. Expense Management
2. Budget Management
3. Expense Analysis

## Expected Outcome

The project helps users organise their expenses and understand their
spending in relation to their monthly budget.
